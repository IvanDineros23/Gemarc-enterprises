Steel Testing Equipment

Steel Testing Equipment & Services

We provide comprehensive steel testing equipment for quality control and material characterization in construction and manufacturing industries. Our equipment meets international standards for steel testing and analysis.

Tensile Testing Machine

Universal testing machines for determining the tensile strength, yield strength, and elongation of steel specimens.

Hardness Testing Equipment

Rockwell, Brinell, and Vickers hardness testing equipment for measuring the hardness of steel materials.

Impact Testing Equipment

Charpy and Izod impact testing machines for evaluating the toughness and impact resistance of steel.

Fatigue Testing Equipment

Fatigue testing machines for determining the fatigue life and endurance limit of steel components.

Our Services Include:

  • Supply of steel testing equipment
  • Material testing and certification services
  • Calibration and verification services
  • Equipment maintenance and repair
  • Technical support and training
