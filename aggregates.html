Aggregates Testing Equipment

Aggregates Testing Equipment & Services

We provide comprehensive aggregates testing equipment and services to ensure the quality and performance of construction materials. Our range includes equipment for testing various properties of aggregates used in construction and infrastructure projects.

Aggregate Impact Testing

Equipment for measuring the impact resistance of aggregates to determine their suitability for construction applications.

Crushing Value Testing

Apparatus for determining the crushing value of aggregates to assess their strength and durability.

Abrasion Testing

Los Angeles abrasion testing equipment to measure the resistance of aggregates to wear and degradation.

Specific Gravity Testing

Equipment for determining the specific gravity and absorption capacity of fine and coarse aggregates.

Our Services Include:

  • Supply of aggregates testing equipment
  • Calibration and verification services
  • Equipment maintenance and repair
  • Technical support and training
