Concrete & Mortar Testing Equipment

We provide comprehensive concrete and mortar testing equipment to ensure quality control in construction projects. Our equipment meets international standards for testing concrete properties and performance characteristics.

CTM-001

Compression Testing Machine

Standard: ASTM C39, EN 12390-3, BS 1881

High-capacity compression testing machines for determining the compressive strength of concrete specimens. Suitable for testing concrete cubes and cylinders.

STA-002

Slump Test Apparatus

Standard: ASTM C143, EN 12350-2, BS 1881-102

Slump cone and related equipment for measuring the workability and consistency of fresh concrete. Essential for quality control in concrete production.

FTM-003

Flexural Testing Machine

Standard: ASTM C78, EN 12390-5, BS 1881-118

Equipment for determining the flexural strength and modulus of rupture of concrete beams. Provides accurate measurement of concrete tensile strength.
