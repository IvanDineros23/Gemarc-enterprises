Concrete & Mortar Testing Equipment

Concrete & Mortar Testing Equipment & Services

We provide comprehensive concrete and mortar testing equipment to ensure quality control in construction projects. Our equipment meets international standards for testing concrete properties and performance characteristics.

Compression Testing Machine

High-capacity compression testing machines for determining the compressive strength of concrete specimens.

Slump Testing

Slump cone and related equipment for measuring the workability and consistency of fresh concrete.

Flexural Strength Testing

Equipment for determining the flexural strength and modulus of rupture of concrete beams.

Non-Destructive Testing

Rebound hammer and ultrasonic pulse velocity equipment for non-destructive testing of concrete.

Our Services Include:

  • Supply of concrete and mortar testing equipment
  • Calibration and verification services
  • Equipment maintenance and repair
  • Technical support and training
