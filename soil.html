Soil Testing Equipment

Soil Testing Equipment & Services

We provide comprehensive soil testing equipment for geotechnical investigation, foundation design, and construction projects. Our equipment meets international standards for soil analysis and testing procedures.

Soil Compaction Testing

Proctor compaction test equipment for determining the optimal moisture content and maximum dry density of soil.

CBR Testing Equipment

California Bearing Ratio testing equipment for evaluating the bearing capacity of soil and subgrade materials.

Triaxial Testing

Triaxial testing apparatus for determining the shear strength parameters of soil specimens.

Consolidation Testing

Consolidation testing equipment for measuring the settlement characteristics of cohesive soils.

Our Services Include:

  • Supply of soil testing equipment
  • Geotechnical investigation services
  • Calibration and verification services
  • Equipment maintenance and repair
  • Technical support and training
